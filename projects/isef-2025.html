IntelliCane: A Deep Learning Cane for Active Obstacle Avoidance & Navigation for the Visually Impaired

Regeneron ISEF 2025 · Grand Award, 3rd place · AAAI Special Award

A deep learning navigation system built into a cane, providing real-time obstacle detection and avoidance for visually impaired users. The project won a Grand Award (3rd place) at the Regeneron International Science & Engineering Fair and received the AAAI Special Award.
