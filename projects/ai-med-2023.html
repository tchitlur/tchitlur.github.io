Early Detection and Synthesis of a 12-Lead ECG Using Density-Based Clustering and GANs

The first version of the question I have been working on since: given part of an ECG, can a model produce the rest of it well enough to detect what a clinician would detect?

The approach here was generative rather than attention-based — density-based clustering to group beat morphologies, then adversarial training to synthesize the missing leads. It set the baseline that the transformer work later had to beat.

I presented at AI-Med 2023 as the youngest of more than eighty pre- and post-doctoral presenters.
