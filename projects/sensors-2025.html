A Morphologically-Aware Transformer Network for High-Fidelity V1–V5 ECG Lead Generation

IEEE Xplore

The six chest leads are where a cardiologist looks to localize ischemia, and they are the ones that depend most on precise electrode placement. If any leads are worth reconstructing well, it is these.

Point-wise error is a misleading target here. A reconstruction can sit close to the ground truth on average and still round off the shape that carries the diagnosis. This network is trained against morphology directly, so the features a reader depends on survive the reconstruction.

Presented at the 24th IEEE Conference on Sensors and published in IEEE Xplore.
