Transformer-Based Framework with Dynamic Multiscale Attention Mechanisms for 12-Lead ECG Synthesis

47th IEEE Engineering in Medicine & Biology Conference (EMBC), 2025 · First author

A peer-reviewed, first-authored paper presented at IEEE EMBC, the largest biomedical engineering conference in the world. The framework uses dynamic multiscale attention to synthesize a full 12-lead ECG, and is indexed in IEEE Xplore and PubMed.
