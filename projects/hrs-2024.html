Comprehensive Validation of Einthoven and Goldberger's Electrocardiographic Lead Systems

HeartRhythm Journal

Einthoven's triangle and Goldberger's augmented leads are the arithmetic that lets three measured limb leads stand in for six. Every reconstruction method inherits those relationships, usually without checking them.

This work tested how well they hold across a large body of real recordings, which matters because a method built on top of a relationship that only approximately holds inherits that error and then adds its own.

Presented and published at the Heart Rhythm Society Annual Meeting, the field's main clinical electrophysiology venue.
