Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair 2024  ·  2024

Synthesis of a Diagnostic-Caliber 12-Lead Electrocardiogram Using a Novel Multi-resolution Transformer

This is where the multi-resolution idea started. Reading an ECG means moving between time scales — the millisecond structure of a QRS complex and the several-hundred-millisecond arc of repolarization — so a model that reconstructs one should be able to do the same.

The target was diagnostic caliber rather than visual similarity: a reconstruction is only useful if the intervals and amplitudes a clinician measures survive it.

The work earned a Grand Award at Regeneron ISEF 2024 (2nd place, Robotics and Intelligent Machines) and was selected through abstract review for HRX 2024. Both IEEE papers above are direct descendants of it.
