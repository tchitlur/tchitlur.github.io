Synthesis of a Diagnostic-Caliber 12-Lead ECG Using a Novel Multi-resolution Transformer

Regeneron ISEF 2024 · Grand Award, 2nd place · Presented at HRX 2024

A multi-resolution transformer for synthesizing a complete, diagnostic-caliber 12-lead ECG. The project won a Grand Award (2nd place) at the Regeneron International Science & Engineering Fair and was presented at HRX 2024.
