Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair 2025  ·  2025

IntelliCane: A Deep Learning Cane for Active Obstacle Avoidance and Navigation for the Visually Impaired

A white cane tells you what is directly in front of the tip. It does not tell you about the bollard at hip height, or the car pulling out of a driveway. IntelliCane adds active obstacle detection and route guidance to the tool people already trust and already carry.

The constraint that shaped the whole project was the form factor. Detection had to run on-device, fast enough to warn before a collision rather than after, and light enough that the cane still handles like a cane.

Presented at Regeneron ISEF 2025, where it received a Grand Award (3rd place, Robotics and Intelligent Machines) and the AAAI Special Award.
