Machine learning for the human heart.

I'm Tanay — a computer science student at Carnegie Mellon and a published ML researcher. I build transformer models that synthesize diagnostic-quality ECG signals, with two first-authored papers in IEEE venues.

About me

I grew up in Portland, Oregon, and started doing research in high school — mostly asking one question: can we generate the parts of a 12-lead ECG we didn't record, well enough for a doctor to trust? Chasing that question led to first-authored papers at IEEE EMBC and IEEE SENSORS, presentations at the Heart Rhythm Society and AI Med, and two ISEF Grand Awards.

Now I'm studying computer science at Carnegie Mellon (Class of 2030). Along the way I've interned as an ML engineer at Hello Alfred.ai, captained a FIRST Tech Challenge robotics team for four years, and run a small dessert business, TnT Treats, since 2019. In 2026 I was named a Coca-Cola Scholar, one of 150 selected from over 110,000 applicants.
